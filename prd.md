# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview
The product is a comprehensive business management platform that streamlines operations, enhances collaboration, and improves decision-making for small to medium-sized enterprises. The platform will offer a suite of integrated modules covering core business functions such as finance, HR, project management, and customer relationship management. The platform will be delivered as a cloud-based solution, accessible through a user-friendly web interface and mobile applications.

## Business Objectives
- Increase operational efficiency and productivity for SMEs
- Provide a centralized, integrated platform to manage all critical business functions
- Improve data-driven decision-making by offering real-time insights and analytics
- Enhance collaboration and communication among team members
- Reduce the administrative burden and IT maintenance costs for SMEs

## Target Users
- Business owners and entrepreneurs of small to medium-sized enterprises
- Department heads and managers across various business functions (finance, HR, sales, operations)
- Employees who need to access and manage business data and workflows

## Functional Requirements

### Epic 1: Financial Management
The financial management module will provide comprehensive tools for SMEs to manage their accounting, budgeting, and reporting processes. This will include features for invoicing, expense tracking, and financial forecasting.

**User Stories:**
- **US-001**: As a finance manager, I want to be able to generate custom financial reports so that I can analyze the company&#x27;s financial performance.
- **US-002**: As a small business owner, I want to be able to track and manage my expenses so that I can maintain control over my company&#x27;s spending.
- **US-003**: As an accountant, I want to be able to create and send invoices to clients directly from the platform so that I can streamline the invoicing process.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The financial management module must support the creation and customization of various financial reports, including income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.
- The expense tracking feature must allow users to easily categorize and record expenses, with the ability to upload receipts and generate expense reports.
- The invoicing functionality must provide templates for creating professional-looking invoices, with the option to send them directly to clients via email.

### Epic 2: Human Resources Management
The HR management module will enable SMEs to efficiently manage their employee-related tasks, such as onboarding, performance reviews, and time-off requests.

**User Stories:**
- **US-004**: As an HR manager, I want to be able to manage employee information and records so that I can maintain up-to-date personnel data.
- **US-005**: As an employee, I want to be able to submit time-off requests and view my leave balances so that I can better plan my time off.
- **US-006**: As a manager, I want to be able to conduct performance reviews and provide feedback to my team members so that I can support their professional development.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The HR management module must allow users to create and maintain employee profiles, including personal information, job details, and employment history.
- The time-off request feature must enable employees to submit leave requests, which can be approved or rejected by their managers, and track their remaining leave balances.
- The performance review functionality must provide templates for conducting regular reviews, with the ability to set goals, provide feedback, and document performance evaluations.

### Epic 3: Project Management
The project management module will help SMEs plan, execute, and monitor their various business projects, ensuring effective collaboration and visibility.

**User Stories:**
- **US-007**: As a project manager, I want to be able to create and manage project schedules, tasks, and milestones so that I can track the progress of my projects.
- **US-008**: As a team member, I want to be able to view my assigned tasks, update their status, and collaborate with my colleagues so that I can contribute effectively to the project.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The project management module must allow users to create and customize project plans, including the ability to define tasks, assign resources, and set deadlines.
- The collaboration features must enable team members to communicate, share files, and update the status of their assigned tasks, with real-time visibility for all project stakeholders.
- The project management module must provide reporting and analytics tools to help managers monitor project performance, identify bottlenecks, and make informed decisions.

## Technical Requirements
- The platform must be built using a modern web technology stack, including a responsive frontend framework and a scalable backend architecture.
- The platform must be designed to be cloud-native, with the ability to seamlessly scale up or down based on user demand.
- The platform must provide secure and role-based access controls, ensuring that users can only access the information and functionalities relevant to their roles.
- The platform must offer integrations with commonly used business tools and systems, such as accounting software, CRM platforms, and productivity apps.
- The platform must have robust data backup and disaster recovery mechanisms to ensure business continuity.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: The platform must be able to handle a minimum of 1,000 concurrent users without any noticeable performance degradation.
- **Security**: The platform must comply with industry-standard security protocols and practices, including end-to-end encryption, multi-factor authentication, and regular security audits.
- **Scalability**: The platform must be able to scale horizontally to support the growing user base and data requirements of SMEs.
- **Usability**: The platform must have a user-friendly and intuitive interface, with a focus on providing a seamless user experience across web and mobile platforms.
- **Reliability**: The platform must have an uptime of at least 99.9% and provide automatic failover and recovery mechanisms to ensure business continuity.

## Success Metrics
- Increase in the number of SMEs using the platform by 50% within the first year of launch.
- Achieve a customer satisfaction rating of at least 4.5 out of 5 stars.
- Reduce the administrative burden for SMEs by at least 30% through the use of the platform.
- Improve decision-making capabilities for SMEs by providing real-time insights and analytics.

## Timeline and Milestones
- Phase 1 (3 months): Develop the core financial management and HR modules.
- Phase 2 (6 months): Implement the project management module and enhance the platform&#x27;s integration capabilities.
- Phase 3 (9 months): Introduce additional modules, such as CRM and inventory management, and optimize the platform&#x27;s performance and scalability.
- Phase 4 (12 months): Launch mobile applications, expand the platform&#x27;s ecosystem, and focus on user engagement and retention.

## Dependencies and Assumptions
- The platform will be developed and hosted on a leading cloud computing platform, such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.
- The platform will require integration with various third-party business applications and services, which may introduce additional dependencies and complexities.
- The platform will be designed to be modular and extensible, allowing for the addition of new features and functionalities in the future.
- The platform will be marketed and sold primarily through a direct sales model, with the potential for partnerships and channel distribution in the future.
